Course Description

This course connects fundamental robotics concepts with practical implementation. Students will study robot structures, coordinate transformations, kinematics, robot modeling, programming, and motion planning. Examples from robotic manipulation and industrial applications provide context for understanding how these elements work together.

Practical work will use the DR-xcobo 2.0 as the course platform, with an emphasis on URDF modeling, Python interfaces, and MoveIt-based motion planning. Students will develop and validate applications in simulation, document their results, and communicate their design decisions. Hardware activities will be arranged according to platform availability and the teaching schedule.
