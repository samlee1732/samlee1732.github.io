    ADAP: Adaptive & Dynamic Arc Padding for predicting seam profiles in Multi-Layer-Multi-Pass robotic welding

    He Wang, Sen Li, Xiaobo Liu, Chengxiao Dong, Fang Wan

    AI Open, vol. 6, pp. 204–219, 2025.

    DOI: 10.1016/j.aiopen.2025.10.003

    Summary

    This study introduces the Adaptive & Dynamic Arc Padding (ADAP) approach. ADAP employs a novel representation of weld bead profiles using image-based boundary and primitive arc geometries defined by arc center and radius.
