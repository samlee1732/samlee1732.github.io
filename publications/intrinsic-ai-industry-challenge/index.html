    Project overview

    In the simulated workcell, a robot handles both ends of a fiber-optic cable. It inserts the SFP transceiver into the designated network card and the SC connector into its socket. The video pairs an overview of the workcell with three wrist-camera views, showing how the connectors line up with their ports. The asMagicForce team’s Newton simulation is linked below.
